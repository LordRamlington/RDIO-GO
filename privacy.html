Introduction At RDIO-GO, developed by CuveyApps, we value and respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information handling when you use the RDIO-GO mobile application and related radio streaming features. 1. Information Collection & Usage We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any personally identifiable information (PII). No Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal details (name, email address, phone number, address) to use the app. No Geolocation Tracking: RDIO-GO does not track, store, or access your device’s GPS or precise location. No Microphone Access or Surroundings Recording: RDIO-GO does not access your microphone or record any ambient audio. The offline recording feature captures only the public audio streams broadcast by online radio stations, and stores them locally on your device. 2. Device Local Storage Your station favorites, equalizer presets, and offline recorded radio broadcasts are saved exclusively to your device's local memory (IndexedDB and HTML5 localStorage). This information never leaves your device and is never uploaded to remote servers. 3. Audio Streams & Network Activity RDIO-GO communicates directly with legitimate public radio streaming servers via standard internet protocols (HTTP/HTTPS). When you play a station, audio packets are fetched directly from that broadcaster’s stream URL. Broadcasters may receive standard technical HTTP request headers (such as your IP address and user-agent string), which is inherent to standard internet operation. 4. Android Device Permissions RDIO-GO requests only the standard permissions necessary for an audio streaming utility: INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to stream live online radio stations and check network status. FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required by Android to keep audio playing when you lock your screen or switch apps, and to display playback controls in the Android notification shade and lockscreen. POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows the app to display playback status, station artwork, and media controls in the system notification shade. WAKE_LOCK: Prevents the device processor from sleeping while audio is actively streaming. REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Provides an optional direct shortcut to Android battery settings so you can select Unrestricted mode to prevent background audio dropouts. 5. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics RDIO-GO may display third-party advertisements (such as Google AdMob or Google AdSense). These third-party ad networks may collect non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) and basic technical diagnostic data to deliver contextual ads in compliance with Google Play's Developer Advertising Policies. You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime in your Android system settings under Settings > Google > Ads. 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance) RDIO-GO is a general-audience radio streaming application. We do not direct the app to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with any personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete it. 7. Security We prioritize user data safety. All radio connections use modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) wherever supported by station broadcasters, and all user preferences remain confined strictly to your device's private application sandbox. 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be published with an updated Effective Date. Your continued use of RDIO-GO after any updates constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. 9.
